Sponsored Research Projects

My research focuses on understanding and improving transportation system performance under real-world conditions.

Socially Compatible Driving

This project explores how automated and human-driven vehicles can better align with prevailing traffic behaviors to improve safety and flow.

  • Defines metrics for social compatibility in traffic streams
  • Evaluates impacts on safety and efficiency
  • Proposes data-driven integration strategies

Environmental Impacts on Highway Performance

This project examines how weather and environmental disruptions affect traffic flow and system reliability.

  • Analyzes real-world traffic and weather data
  • Identifies performance degradation patterns
  • Supports operational response strategies
