Sponsored Research Projects

My research focuses on improving transportation system performance and resilience through data-driven methods, with applications in traffic operations, safety analysis, and intelligent transportation systems.

Socially Compatible Driving with Automated Vehicles

This project investigates how automated vehicles can better align with prevailing human driving behaviors to improve safety and traffic flow. I develop metrics for evaluating "social compatibility" in mixed traffic streams and propose data-driven strategies for integrating AVs into existing roadway environments.

Automated Vehicles Traffic Flow Safety Behavioral Analysis

Environmental Impacts on Highway Performance

Using machine learning techniques applied to real-world traffic and weather data, this work develops novel weather severity classifications based on observed speed reductions. The goal is to support operational response strategies that proactively manage traffic degradation under adverse conditions.

Weather & Traffic Machine Learning Speed Prediction TSMO

Post-Incident Traffic Speed Recovery

This research develops predictive models for how traffic speeds recover following highway crashes, using probe vehicle data. The work spans from identifying key influencing factors to building systematic, data-driven tools for real-time operational decision support.

Incident Management Probe Data Speed Recovery Prediction

Driver-Assistance-Enabled Electric Vehicles: Safety, Mobility & Environment

An empirical study quantifying the safety, mobility, and environmental impacts of ADAS-equipped electric vehicles on Arizona highways. This project bridges transportation electrification and driver assistance technology to understand their combined real-world effects.

Electric Vehicles ADAS Safety Emissions

Split Failure Detection at Signalized Intersections

Leveraging multisource data — including controller event logs — to investigate the occurrence and contributing factors of split failures at signalized intersections. Findings inform better signal timing and operational strategies for urban arterials.

Signal Operations Controller Data Arterials
