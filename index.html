Gabriel Geffen

PhD Candidate in Transportation Engineering

About

I am a PhD candidate in Transportation Engineering focused on improving transportation system performance through data-driven methods. My research integrates transportation operations, safety, and intelligent transportation systems (ITS) to better understand and respond to real-world disruptions.

My work emphasizes system-level analysis, combining empirical data with advanced modeling techniques to evaluate how environmental conditions, emerging vehicle technologies, and operational strategies impact roadway performance.

Research Interests

  • Transportation Operations and Traffic Flow
  • Transportation Safety and Risk Analysis
  • Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)
  • Data-Driven Modeling and Prediction
  • Alternative Fuel Vehicles and System Impacts

Current Work

My current research investigates how environmental disruptions and evolving vehicle technologies influence highway performance, with an emphasis on developing data-driven tools to support real-time operational decision-making.
